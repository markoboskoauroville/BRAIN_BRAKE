# WHAT EVERY SCENE HAS TO SOUND LIKE

Baba, 5.9.2026. A brief for the scratch track, one scene at a time. Written from
the film, not from the myNoise catalogue.

**This is half of the job.** I know what each scene needs to sound like. Claude
Code has been through the whole myNoise site and knows which SEARCH TERMS
actually return something and which of the three-word *Not an AI* combinations
produce anything usable. The keywords below are my guesses and are marked
UNVERIFIED until he confirms them, because a keyword that returns nothing wastes
an hour and a keyword that returns the wrong thing wastes a scene.

**Every scene gets STEMS, never a mix.** One take per slider, so the balance is
made in the edit.

**One rule under all of it:** the film is quiet. It is a boy alone inside his own
head. If a bed is doing anything you can point at, it is too loud.

---

## 0. TITLE

**Sound.** Almost nothing. Room tone, and the sense of a held breath. The film
should feel like it started before you noticed.

**Not.** No music, no swell, no whoosh. Nothing announcing a film.

UNVERIFIED: `room tone`, `silence`, `air`

---

## 1. THE MARATHON RUNNER

**Sound.** An ordinary morning outdoors, and this is the most important bed in
the film: birds, wind through an avenue of trees, gravel, a road somewhere far
off. The collapse has to land in a completely normal world.

Then it thins. When he runs out of frame and the page empties, **take the bed
away entirely** and leave paper silence. When Manan walks in with the glass, it
does not come back. The world is gone and we are looking at a drawing now.

**Not.** No crowd, no race, no sport. There is no event; there is one man.

UNVERIFIED: `forest wind birds`, `morning forest`, `woodland`, `gravel`,
`distant traffic`

---

## 2. HE TAKES THE QUESTION OUTSIDE

**Sound.** Suburban daylight. Street, air, a little traffic, distance. The last
ordinary place in the film.

**Not.** No city. Not busy. He is thinking.

UNVERIFIED: `street`, `suburb`, `distant traffic`, `open air`

---

## 3. HE TESTS IT ON THE BICYCLE

**Sound.** Movement and air. Wind past the ears, tyres, the road. Young and a
little reckless.

**Not.** No music driving it. The energy comes from the cut.

UNVERIFIED: `wind`, `cycling`, `road`, `air movement`

---

## 4. HE RACES HIMSELF

**Sound.** Indoors, and the room gets smaller. Trainer whine, breath, a laptop
fan, a fluorescent hum. **Breath is the lead instrument here and everything else
sits under it.**

**Not.** No outdoors at all. The world has shrunk to a room.

UNVERIFIED: `room hum`, `fan`, `machine hum`, `breathing`

---

## 5. HE CLOSES HIS EYES

**Sound.** Everything leaves. **Take the sound away BEFORE the picture goes**,
which is the note already on the frame. A held low tone under it, or nothing.

This is the hinge of the film and it should feel like relief.

**Not.** No cue, no sting, no rising anything. It is a release, not an event.

UNVERIFIED: `low drone`, `sub bass`, `deep hum`

---

## 6. THE VORTEX, UP

**Sound.** Rising air and cloth. He is carried, not falling, and the sound has
to carry that: air moving past, fabric, a low turning tone. No impact, because
nothing hits anything.

**Not.** No wind howl and no storm. It is not violent. It is being taken.

UNVERIFIED: `wind tunnel`, `air`, `drone`, `cloth`, `rising tone`

---

## 7. THE HOUSE OF THE BODY, AND THE KEY DOOR

**Sound.** Inside a body that is also a building. Room tone with a pulse under
it, far off, felt rather than heard. A lift. Machinery that is calm and well
maintained. Footsteps on stone at the hip, then no footsteps at all once the
lift takes him, because he is being carried.

**One unbroken move means one unbroken bed.** No cuts in the sound either.

**Not.** No drips, no horror, no cave. This place is working properly.

UNVERIFIED: `heartbeat`, `machine room`, `hum`, `lift`, `stone`, `body`

---

## 8. HE MEETS VIVEKA, AND THE TWO THEORIES

**Sound.** A quiet control room. Near silence, a very low equipment tone, and
room. The dialogue carries the whole scene, so the bed exists only to make the
silence sound deliberate rather than empty.

**Not.** No dials, no beeping, no computers. He does not need any of that; three
trackballs and a hologram say it already.

UNVERIFIED: `control room`, `quiet hum`, `air conditioning`, `deep space`

---

## 9. THE CONCLUSION, AND GOODBYE

**Sound.** The same room, thinner still. Nothing new arrives.

**Not.** No resolution chord. Nobody won.

UNVERIFIED: `room tone`, `quiet`

---

## 10. HE LETS THE KEY GO, AND IT FALLS

**Sound.** Air, and the film running backwards. Something reversed, so the ear
knows the direction of travel without being told. **One clean gold sound as the
key leaves his fingers**, and nothing else that bright anywhere in the film.

The strip should have a texture as it races past. Film through a projector is
the honest reference.

**Not.** No wind roar. He is falling with the key, so relative to it there is
almost no air at all.

UNVERIFIED: `reverse`, `projector`, `film reel`, `air`, `bell`, `chime`

---

## 11. HE CATCHES IT

**Sound.** Stillness, and one small solid contact. The key lands in a hand.
Everything around it stops.

**Not.** No music. This is the quietest moment in the film and the only one that
has a real sound in it.

UNVERIFIED: `room tone`, `silence`

---

## 12. CREDITS, AND THE DEDICATION

**Sound.** The end credits music, already composed. Cloud under it if anything.

**Not.** Nothing new. The film is over.
