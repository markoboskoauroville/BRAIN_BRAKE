VERSION FIVE · THE MENTOR

Six scenes · thirty six panels · Manan on camera throughout · 11.8.2026

Manan is the film. He is on camera in all six scenes, speaking, in his own room, and the drawn world supports him instead of carrying him. The structure is the oldest one there is: a boy with a question, and a teacher who is not what he expected.

Why this version exists, and it is the rubric rather than taste. The competition scores four things equally: engagement, illumination, creativity, difficulty. Illumination asks whether the viewer developed genuine understanding, and a film with no words cannot compete on a quarter of the score. Judges are reading the student and not the film. A past winner recommends the student be on screen for at least forty percent of the running time. Version four had him on for eight seconds out of a hundred and twenty. Read it against version four and the original.
