# THE BRAIN BRAKE, SCENE ONE
## Notes on the renders, 5 September 2026

Written out clip by clip from the two review videos. In the videos a sentence often starts
over one shot and finishes over the next, and many comments are general rules rather than
notes on a shot. Here the rules are stated once and every other note is attached to the clip
it is actually about. Clip names were read off the burned-in title bar, so they are exact.

---

## RULES FOR EVERY SHOT

**NOTHING MAY BE ADDED TO A SHOT**

Do not generate any element that is not in the reference frame. If something extra has appeared, take it out. Added elements pull the eye in the wrong direction and this is the note Baba repeats most.

> Ne dodavati nijedan element koji nije u referentnom okviru. Ako se nešto pojavilo, ukloniti.

**NO APPEARING OR BUILD-ON EFFECTS**

Elements arriving one at a time, words materialising, build-on animation: all removed, to keep the animation simple and get through the film faster.

> Efekt pojavljivanja, element po element, riječi koje se pojavljuju: sve to maknuti.

**SCENE ONE ONLY, UNTIL IT IS FINISHED**

Do not work ahead. Everything else waits.

> Fokus samo na prvu scenu dok je ne završimo.

**THE PAPER COLOUR IS THE SAME THROUGH THE WHOLE FILM**

Do not try to match it per shot. Baba keys the finished shots onto one background at the very end, so leave it alone and get everything else right first.

> Boja papira ista kroz cijeli film. To se radi na kraju, keyanjem na novu pozadinu.

**THE LIVE FOOTAGE FOR COMPOSITING IS ON GOOGLE DRIVE**

The plates you need are there already.

> Snimke za kompoziting su na G-disku.
