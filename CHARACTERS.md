# CHARACTERS

**Nobody in this film may be generated without the reference named here.**

Written 19.8.2026, after a generation of Coach Brain came back as a doctor in a lab coat with an ID
badge. The character sheets existed. They were simply not used, because nothing said they had to be.
Now something does.

---

## THE RULE

1. **A character is only ever generated with its locked single figure reference from this file.**
   Not from the frame it appears in, not from memory, not from a description alone.
2. **Never reference a model sheet.** The sheets below are four views on one page. `nanobanana.md`
   rule 3: a multi panel reference imposes its grid on the output. **Sheets are for humans. The
   single figure file is for the machine.**
3. **The costume line below goes into every prompt, verbatim.** It is short on purpose.
4. **If a frame needs a character that is not in this file, stop and ask Baba.** Do not invent one.
   That is exactly how a doctor appeared in a film that has no doctor in it.
5. After generating, **check the costume against the line below before shipping.** Wrong clothes is a
   reject, not a note.

## COACH BRAIN

The coach inside your head. Calm, warm, never panicked, never a villain.

**Machine reference:** `assets/BRAND/COACH_BRAIN_ON_SET.png` — single figure, transparent.
**Human sheet, do not feed to the model:** `assets/sb/char-coach-brain.jpg` — four views.

**Costume line, use verbatim:**
> a friendly cartoon brain for a head, a thin headset microphone at his cheek, a zip up tracksuit
> jacket with a stripe down each sleeve, matching tracksuit trousers, a lanyard round his neck, and
> white trainers

He is **not** a doctor, **not** a scientist, and wears **no** lab coat and **no** badge. That
sentence is for the human reading this file. Never put it in a prompt, because naming a thing
summons it. In the prompt, describe only the tracksuit.

## THE RUNNER

The marathon man of 1923. Drawn in graphite, singlet with the number 27.

### THE FACE, LOCKED 28.8.2026

**Why this exists.** Until today the only description of this man's face in the whole production was
nine words — *long face, heavy brow, deep set eyes, hair swept back* — repeated verbatim in five
documents. That is a category, not a face. The model filled the gap with the most familiar face that
fits the category, which is how the frames ended up looking like a well known actor and why he
changed between every version. **A face that is not specified will be invented, differently, every
time.**

Read this as one block into any prompt that draws him. Do not paraphrase it and do not shorten it.

> A gaunt man of forty five with a long narrow skull and a high flat forehead. The eyes are set wide
> apart and deep under a heavy straight brow ridge, with two vertical creases cut between the brows.
> The nose is long and straight with a flattening at the bridge, as though it was broken years ago
> and set badly. The cheekbones are high and wide and there are deep hollows beneath them. The jaw is
> narrow and the chin is small and square. The mouth is wide and thin with the corners turned down.
> The hair is dark and fine, cut short at the sides and swept straight back off the forehead, receding
> at the temples. The skin is weathered, a man who has spent his life outdoors.

**The three that carry the identity** and must never be dropped from a prompt: **the flattened bridge
of the nose**, **the wide-set deep eyes under a straight brow**, and **the narrow jaw under wide
cheekbones**. Those three are what stop him becoming a generic gaunt man again.

**Sheets:** face sheet and body sheet, being made 28.8.2026. `assets/sb/char-runner.jpg` is the old
human sheet and `assets/REFERENCES/RUNNER.jpg` is the four view body sheet, neither of which contains
a face at a size that can hold an identity.

## WHAT IS MISSING

Only Coach Brain currently has a single figure machine reference. The runner, the workers and the
muscle have sheets but no single figure file. **Before generating any of them, make one**: crop or
generate a single figure on plain cream from the sheet, save it into `assets/BRAND/`, and add it
here. A sheet used directly will bring its grid with it.
