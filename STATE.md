## THE PROTOCOL

**Opening a session.** Read this file first, before doing anything else. It replaces asking what happened.

**Closing a session.** Update the three living lists below, then commit and push. A session that ends without
writing here did not happen, because the other worker will never know about it.

**When something becomes a rule.** Put it in CONVENTIONS immediately, in one line. A rule discovered and not
written down will be broken within three scenes.

**When you disagree with what is written here.** Say so, do not quietly work around it. This file is the
argument of record.

## THE FILM IN ONE SENTENCE

A runner with nothing left produces an impossible sprint, a boy goes looking for where it came from, expects
to find an empty tank and finds a full one, and a door that somebody closed on purpose.

**The limit is a setting, not a wall.**

## CONVENTIONS, THE RULES THAT DO NOT GET RENEGOTIATED

**Style law.** Graphite pencil on warm cream paper, sixteen by nine, thin dark ruled border on every frame.
Humans are drawn realistically. Cartoon is reserved for Coach Brain and the Muscle, the two characters who are
ideas rather than people, so a cartoon appearing always means something. Manan alone is a real photograph
composited into the drawing, always, without exception.

**Colour.** Graphite, cream, gold, red. Gold only on the key and the light of understanding. Red only on
gauges. Nothing else carries colour. Cyan was dropped.

**The gap.** When the brake opens, the needle stops short of the red with visible white between them. Never to
one hundred. This is the moral architecture of the film and it is not negotiable for excitement.

**No villain.** Coach Brain is a careful guardian doing a reasonable job with incomplete information. The
moment an audience has somebody to blame, they stop thinking.

**Six threads run underneath.** The key rises, the magnifying glass falls in exact opposition, the number 27
recurs at every scale, workers appear outside the body before inside it, the needle climbs, the light travels
from flat overcast to pure white. None of it is ever spoken.

**Banned vocabulary.** meditation, energy, spirit, universe, healing, divine, consciousness, karma. Each one
sorts the audience into camps at the door. Use breath, still, attention, permission, decision, belief,
trained. Build a well, not a temple.

**Working with the image tool.** Reference sheets are fed in as URLs, never uploaded again. One correction per
pass, phrased as keep everything and change this, because a list of changes makes it rebuild rather than edit.
Name physical materials, wool nap, pores, hair strands, leather, never the word photograph. When a correction
pass goes wrong, regenerate the previous pass before writing another prompt.

**Files.** Version number first in every filename, underscores throughout, `_i` and `_u` for install and
uninstall. Version label in every app carries the account letter, currently `(a)`.

**Tools.** No command line switches anywhere, ever. Single keypress menus, raw input, no Enter, options shown
as bracketed letters. No typing where a file picker will do. Every server suppresses the Flask warning, scans
for a free port, prints a coloured block logo, and answers to q, o and b.

## THE THING WORTH REMEMBERING WHILE WORKING

The surface of this film is a mechanical explanation of fatigue and that surface is complete on its own.
Everything else travels underneath, carried by objects and composition, and is never said aloud. A viewer who
wants only the science gets only the science. A viewer who feels the rest takes it home without being told,
and because they brought it themselves, they trust it.

Say the science. Show the rest.

## IMAGE GENERATION

**Read `nanobanana.md` in the repo root before writing any image prompt.** It is the
accumulated knowledge of how Nano Banana Pro actually behaves: the four prompt rules, the
reference sheet discipline, the failure-mode table, the prompt skeleton and the order of work
that stops credits being burned on re-rolls. It was written after that knowledge was lost once
and had to be dug back out of old sessions. Keep it current: every new failure gets a dated
line under NEW FINDINGS, and anything that holds twice moves up into the rules.

## WRITING DOCUMENTS

**Read `documents.md` in the repo root before writing any document.** The rule is that a reader
should feel the film rather than read it: short, emotional, one page per scene, no conversation
recaps and no next-steps lists. One exception, and it is a real one: the principles behind
composition, colour, numbers, light, metaphor and structure may run long, provided they stay
interesting and never repeat, and they live in their own document rather than in the scene pages.
