HERO FRAMES, VERSION THREE

The eight scenes of the film we are shooting, one image each. Final artwork. Every frame carries the story of its own scene and one share of the threads that run underneath the whole film.

CHARACTER REFERENCE SHEETS

The law of the film. Every frame is drawn against these, which is why the whole thing reads as one hand rather than a set of separate pictures. Humans are drawn realistically. Cartoon is reserved for the two characters who are ideas rather than people. Manan alone is always a real photograph.

LOCATION REFERENCE SHEETS

Each space drawn empty first, before any story frame, so every later shot inherits the same architecture and light.

ARCHIVE, EARLIER VERSIONS

Frames drawn for version one and version two before the story shape and the style law were settled. Kept for reference only. They are not the film.
