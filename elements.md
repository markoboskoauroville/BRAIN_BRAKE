# THE FIVE ELEMENTS IN THE BRAIN BRAKE

A standing rule document. Read it when a scene is finished but feels thin and nobody can say why.

The frame comes from tantra, ayurveda and Chinese medicine, and it is borrowed here as a checklist for
wholeness, not as a cosmology. The historical claims made for it elsewhere are shakier than its
enthusiasts pretend and none of them are needed. What is useful is that each element names a different
register in which a person experiences anything, so if one is missing the audience feels an absence
they cannot name.

    EARTH   weight     bodies, materials, consequence
    WATER   care       emotion, tending, softening
    FIRE    want       will, drive, heat, conflict
    WIND    thought    the idea, the structure, the question, movement
    ETHER   space      sound, silence, the unexplained, air around things

---

## THE HONEST DIAGNOSIS OF THIS FILM

Run it properly and the film is short of exactly one element.

**Earth is everywhere.** A body at its limit. Graphite, which is itself a mineral, on paper. Chalk on a
blackboard. The physical fact of a calf muscle. The film is unusually strong here.

**Fire is the subject.** Will, the surge, the lever, competition, cream burning to gold. Also strong.

**Wind is the argument.** Hill's theory, the Coach's calculation, the question the film asks, the
travelling of the view from leg to head. Strong.

**Ether is the silence.** No words anywhere in two minutes. The white at the end. The space around a
single figure on open paper. Present, and worth protecting when the temptation to add narration comes.

**Water was almost absent.** It was represented by sweat and by nothing else, which is water in its
least generous form, and sweat is a by-product of fire rather than water in its own right. A film with
this little water is admired and not loved.

---

## WHAT WAS DONE ABOUT IT

Water gets a proper entrance, and it is where Manan arrives.

At the water station the runner takes a bottle and tips it over his head. In those flying drops the
boy first appears. The element and the meaning are the same thing here, because **water is compassion
and the whole story turns on somebody stopping for a man nobody else stopped for.** Neither has to be
mentioned.

This also fixes a craft problem. Sweat carrying a face was faintly unpleasant, and the frame kept
coming back as soap bubbles because clean spheres are what the model draws when asked for water on
skin. Poured water is genuinely round and airborne, so the model and the physics finally agree.

**Fire meets water at the exact right moment.** He pours the water at the point of maximum heat, and
every tradition in the frame says the same thing about leadership, that fire without water does not
last. That is also the argument of scene 7, where the lever goes to ninety five and never to a hundred.

## THE RULE GOING FORWARD

Every scene should be checked against the five before it is called finished. Not every scene needs all
five, but the film as a whole must span them, and no scene should be built from one alone.

Where an element is thin, the fix is almost never to add a symbol of it. It is to find the thing
already in the scene that carries it and let it be seen. Sweat was a symbol of water. A boy in a
poured drop is water.

he cannot explain, which is pure wind. It needs weight or feeling under it. Unresolved.
