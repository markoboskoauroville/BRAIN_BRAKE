# V6 BOARD DEFECT REGISTER

**Opened 11.8.2026 on Marko's instruction: the boards go out error free.** Every defect found in a full
pass over the eight sheets, in priority order. Fixed one at a time with the editor, single panel repair,
so a fault costs one panel rather than a sheet.

**The rule behind the priority.** A defect that states something untrue about the science or the story
comes first. A defect that a viewer would notice comes second. A defect only the director would notice
comes last, and some of those are left alone.

---

## PRIORITY ONE, WRONG MEANING

    S8 p3   THE LEVER IS UPSIDE DOWN. Ball at the bottom of the slot with the shaft
            rising out of it, gripped in the middle. On LEVER.jpg the ball is the
            grip and sits on top of the shaft. Marko caught this.
            Also: no scale and no numbers on it, and the hand is an adult's in a
            cuffed sleeve when it should be the boy's. This is his moment.

    S6 p2   The ball already sits high, close to 100, BEFORE he eases it. Panel 3
            then shows it at 95. So the lever appears to travel DOWNWARD across the
            two panels, which reverses the meaning of the scene. Panel 2 should sit
            low, well below 95.

    S2 p2   The chalkboard chain reads RUN FASTER, OXYGEN, LACTATE, FATIGUE, STOP
            with nothing marking it as the old theory. Correct as a teaching beat
            because the stamp cracks two panels later, but the chalkboard alone will
            be screenshotted. Add the year 1923 chalked at the top of the board.

## PRIORITY TWO, VISIBLE TO A VIEWER

    S1 p2   The runner is drawn as an old man, sixty or more, deep lines and grey
            hair. Everywhere else in the film he is lean and about thirty five.

    S3      The boy is seen from behind or tiny in five of six panels. The scene has
            no shot of his face at all, which is a problem in a film scored on the
            student being present. Panel 6 should turn him to us.
