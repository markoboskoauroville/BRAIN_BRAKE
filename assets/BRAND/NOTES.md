# PRODUCTION MARK, DEVELOPMENT NOTES

The mark for the crew group and for the production. Two lines of attack, one abandoned, one working.
Written down because the failures are more instructive than the successes, and because the next film will
face the same problem.

Date 7.8.2026.

---

## LINE ONE, THE ABSTRACT MARK, ABANDONED AFTER NINE ATTEMPTS

The idea was to build the mark from the film's two objects, the magnifying glass and the golden key, the
instrument of looking and the instrument of opening, left brain and right brain.

**What was tried, in order.**

1. Circle as brain, glass on one hemisphere, key on the other. Flat, and the objects sat on their own colour
   so nothing contrasted.
2. Reversed colours, silver over warm, gold over cool, both tilted into a shallow X. Better in theory, and
   the tool kept refusing or drifting.
3. Faded brain sketch behind, paper split on a diagonal, spiral hatching. Still two objects lying beside one
   another, so still a catalogue page.
4. **The real idea.** The lens and the bow of the key are the *same circle*, and through the glass that ring
   reads as a keyhole and as the film's dial. One shape, three meanings. This was the only genuinely good
   idea in the whole line.
5. Rendered well once, then the tool nested two dials inside each other, dropped the key's ring and pushed
   the needle into the red, which the film cannot say.
6. Cleaned back to a single circle with the sketched gauge, the keyhole and the blurred needle. The centre
   was correct and both handles were missing.
7. Handles added. Correct, and dead, because two objects joined at a circle can only ever read as a crest,
   static and symmetrical.
8. Free fall version, both objects tumbling in space toward the drawn gauge, chaos above resolving to order
   below. Did not land.

**Why it was abandoned.** Not because any single render failed, but because the whole line was asking a
symbol to do a character's job. A mark that has to be decoded is the wrong object for a chat window forty
pixels wide.

**Worth keeping.** The shared circle idea, lens and key ring and gauge as one shape, is still good. It may
belong in the film itself rather than on a mark, possibly as an insert.

## LINE TWO, THE CHARACTER, WORKING

Coach Brain as the production mark. Landed on the first attempt, which is the lesson.

A face is the strongest focal element available, before any technique is applied. It also carries tone
instantly, which a symbol cannot, and it survives a circular crop.

**Attempts, all archived in `tries/`.**

| File | Pose | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| `A_SELFIE_THUMBS_UP` | Selfie, grinning, thumbs up | Rejected, the thumbs up made him a mascot asking to be liked |
| `B_BOSS_ARMS_FOLDED` | Arms folded, chain, low angle | Kept as a candidate, promoted to `COACH_BRAIN_BOSS` |
| `C_THRONE_SWIVEL_CHAIR` | Reclining in the swivel chair | Rejected |
| `D_HANDOVER_KEY_OFFERED` | Key held out toward the viewer | Rejected, though it carried the film's idea best |
| `E_GRILL_GRIPPING_CHAIN` | Gripping the chain, hip hop pose | Rejected |

**Current direction.** Vanity rather than power. He is on set, being filmed, standing too straight, beaming,
smoothing his tracksuit for the camera, delighted to be in a real film. Camera, softbox and boom sketched
faintly at the very edges of the frame so the circular crop cannot damage anything that matters.

## RULES LEARNED, FOR THE NEXT MARK

- **A character beats a symbol** for anything that appears small and beside a name. Symbols need explanation,
  faces do not.
- **Two objects side by side never interact.** If two things must relate, they have to share a shape, occupy
  different distances, or one must act on the other.
- **Design for the circular crop from the first prompt.** Borders, dividing lines and anything reaching the
  corners will be sliced. Keep everything inside a central circle with a wide margin.
- **One accent colour only.** Gold on graphite. Every attempt that added a second and third tint got worse.
- **Iterating on a broken structure is wasted work.** When the geometry is wrong rather than the detail,
  start again from a described build order instead of correcting the render.
- **The tool has no opinion about meaning.** It pushed the needle into the red without hesitating, which
  would have turned a careful guardian into a reckless one.
