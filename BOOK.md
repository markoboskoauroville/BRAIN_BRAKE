## WHY THIS FILE IS A POINTER AND NOT THE BOOK

**2.9.2026.** This file used to hold the book. Part Two was written here first, then Part One was
written later and pushed to `BRAIN_BRAKE_BOOK` instead, and neither copy was ever reconciled. So for
several hours there were two files with the same name in two repositories: this one with 1,982 words
and the other with 3,106.

**That nearly cost the whole of Part One.** A correction to chapter nine was prepared against this
file and was about to be written on top of the complete book. It was caught only because the
paragraph count fell from 83 to 49 on the way past.

**Nothing was broken and nothing errored.** Both files opened. Both read like a book. The shorter one
simply was not all of it, and nothing about it said so.

**So the rule that applies here is the one in `MANTRA_MANIFEST/modules/handoff.md`: two documents,
one truth. If two places describe the same thing, one of them must point at the other rather than
repeat it. Repetition is where drift starts.**
