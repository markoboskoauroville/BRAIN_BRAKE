# SCENE 3, THE ARRIVAL

**0:26 to 0:33.** New scene. The film is now nine scenes, not eight.

---

## WHAT HAPPENS

The view snaps outward. What filled the paper a second ago was one drop of sweat, and in a single fast
pull it becomes skin, then a face, then a shoulder, then the whole man, small and alone on open paper
with the finish line still crossing behind him. He is held mid stride where the reversal left him.

Manan walks in from the side and stops beside him, and for the first time in the film he is at human
scale, the same height as the runner, a real photographed boy standing next to a drawing. He does not
look at the man's face. He looks at his calf.

He raises the magnifying glass to the leg. The lens fills the paper. We go through it.

---

## WHY THIS SCENE EXISTS AT ALL

Scene 2 has no wide shot in it by design, which leaves the audience with an accurate feeling and no
geography. That debt has to be paid before the film can go anywhere, and paying it is a job worth
seven seconds of its own rather than a job squeezed onto the end of the reversal.

It also gives Manan a proper entrance. He was glimpsed in scene 2, inverted, dozens of times, inside
water. Here he is a whole person standing on the same ground as the man he has come to examine. A
character introduced twice, first as a hint and then as a fact, is stronger than a character
introduced once, and the audience does the connecting themselves.

**The snap is the point.** A slow pull back would be a camera move. A fast one is an event, and it
lands as relief after eight seconds of held breath. It is the exhale of the first act.

---

## THE THREE FRAMES

**3A, the pull back.** Done. The runner suddenly small on open paper with graphite streaking outward
from him toward every edge. There is no blur in this film. A pencil does not blur, it streaks.

**It was not generated, it was built.** Nano Banana inherits subject scale from its references and
cannot be talked out of it, and it cannot organise a field of streaks around a point either. So `V4_3A`
was assembled in the container from `V4_1C`, scaled to a third of frame height and set on open paper
with the streaks and the ruled line drawn in. Continuity is therefore exact rather than approximate,
because it is the same figure and not a redrawing of him. Any pull back or push in later in the film
should be made the same way.

`assets/V4/attempts/2B_a1_face_hanging_sweat.png` still serves as an in-between of this move, since the
pull passes through that framing on the way out.

**3B, the two of them.** They face each other. Manan comes in from the **right**, close to us and
filling the height of the paper, and the runner hangs in the air at the **far left**, cropped by the
border, still facing right into the frame as he has in every frame of the film. Manan is looking down
at the calf, not at the face.

**The size question, settled.** They are at their true proportions, a fourteen year old boy and a grown
man, and the difference in how much paper each of them occupies comes from **depth rather than height**.
Manan is nearer. That solves what the first attempt got wrong, where he was scaled to the man and
started reading as a small adult.

It also puts him where the tool can render him: large, single and in the foreground. His entrance and
the model's one reliable condition happen to be the same thing.

This is the empathy frame of the scene, and it comes from the fact that a boy has walked up to a man
that nobody else stopped for.

**3C, into the lens. The calf is not in the frame, the calf IS the frame.** A single diagonal mass of
muscle running from the lower left corner to the upper right, with bare paper left only in the top left
and bottom right corners. No knee, no ankle, no shoe. At that distance a leg stops being a leg and
becomes a landscape, which is exactly right for the moment before we go inside it.

The glass sits in the middle of that mass with muscle surrounding the circle on every side. **The calf
frames the lens.** Two attempts failed by having a large glass float over a small leg on open paper,
which reads as somebody demonstrating a prop rather than examining a man.

**We see the back of Manan's head.** Not his profile. He is close to us in the lower right, turned away,
the back of the deerstalker and his hair and the shoulder of the coat, his arm reaching out to hold the
glass against the muscle. This puts the audience behind his shoulder looking where he is looking, which
is the whole business of the scene, and it also keeps his face for the frames that need it.

**And inside the circle is not a bigger calf. It is the laboratory, and the laboratory is a private
room and not a classroom.** A cluttered desk buried in loose paper, more paper on the floor, one lamp,
and a man with wild white hair sitting with his chin in his hand, stuck. He is not teaching anybody. He
is a person alone with a problem he has not solved yet, which is the only way scene 5 can work, because
a man who is lecturing cannot be surprised and a man who is thinking can.

That look carries forward into scene 4 and defines the character.

**And the technical reason for it.** This was always the plan, that
whatever is in the lens is the first inch of scene 4, and it has now become the only version that can
be made at all, because the model cannot magnify anything. Magnification is a transformation. Putting
different content inside a circle is an arrangement, and arrangement is the one thing it does reliably.
The constraint and the story arrived at the same frame.

The circle also pays off scene 2. The audience met a round lens as water with a boy inside it, and here
is the same circle as his instrument with a room inside it. Nothing needs to be said.

## THE THREAD THAT NEEDS A DECISION

The old rule was that the magnifying glass descends through the film in opposition to the rising key.
The key has moved to the Coach's chain and the leg is now below and the head above, so a monotonic
descent no longer matches what the film does.

Proposed replacement: **the glass falls to the leg and then rises to the head**, which is the film's
own movement, real below and invented above, and it is put down in the last scene. Marko to confirm.

---

## FOR THE ANIMATOR

The whole scene is one continuous move: out fast, hold, then in fast. Two accelerations with a
stillness between them, and the stillness is where the audience meets Manan. Do not fill it.
