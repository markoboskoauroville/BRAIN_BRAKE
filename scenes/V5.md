# scenes/V5.md

**VERSION FIVE. THE MENTOR.** Opened 11.8.2026.

Six scenes. Manan on camera in every one of them, speaking, in his own room. The drawn world is a
supporting player rather than the film. Animation load falls by more than half.

---

## 1. WHY THIS VERSION EXISTS, AND IT IS NOT A CHANGE OF TASTE

The competition was researched properly on 11.8.2026 and version four is beautiful and is the wrong bet.

**The official rubric has four criteria of equal weight: engagement, illumination, creativity,
difficulty.** Illumination asks whether the video explained the subject clearly enough that a viewer
develops genuine understanding. **A film with no words in it cannot compete on that criterion**, and it
is a quarter of the score. The rules also require entries to be in English, including scripts and
voiceovers, which assumes a voice.

**And the judges are reading the student, not the film.** Every source says the same thing: they are
trying to work out whether the understanding is real or rehearsed, and production polish cannot rescue an
unclear explanation. Hillary Diane Andales, who won it, wrote that entries showing their creator do
measurably better and recommends the student be on screen for at least forty percent of the running time.
Version four has him on screen for about eight seconds out of a hundred and twenty.

**So the silence, which is the best thing about version four, is the thing that would lose.** That is
worth writing down plainly, because it was a good idea and it took two weeks.

---

## 2. THE SHAPE, TAKEN FROM STAR WARS AND NOT HIDDEN

Marko's call, and it is right, because this is the oldest working structure for exactly this problem: a
boy who has a question, and a teacher who is not what he expected.

    the call            an impossible thing is witnessed and cannot be explained
    the old answer      the received wisdom, honoured, and found to be incomplete
    the mentor          small, comic, unimpressive, and completely right
    the lesson          counterintuitive, and about limits
    the disbelief       "I don't believe it." "That is why you fail."
    the demonstration   the mentor does the impossible thing, calmly
    the return          the student does it himself, in his own words

**Coach Brain is Yoda.** A cartoon brain in a tracksuit holding a coffee mug is exactly the right kind of
unimpressive: you underestimate him, and then he lifts the ship out of the swamp. He is not a villain and
never was. He is a teacher with a better model of the situation than his student has.

**Manan is the disciple, and that is the whole reason the version works for this competition.** A student
being taught on camera is a student demonstrating understanding on camera. The structure and the rubric
want the same thing.

---

## 3. THE TWO WAY CROSSING, WHICH IS THE FILM'S SIGNATURE

Version four had one direction only: Manan entered the drawn world through a lens. Version five goes both
ways, and it costs almost nothing.

    Manan reaches INTO the drawing        a real hand entering the graphite world,
                                          touching a drawn object, moving it
    the drawing reaches OUT to Manan      a pencil line running onto his real desk,
                                          Coach Brain stepping off the page into his room

**And the crossing is the argument.** The film is about the boundary between what is physically true and
what is decided, and a boundary that can be crossed from both sides is that idea made visible. The moment
Manan puts his own hand on the drawn lever is the thesis of the film and the climax of the story at the
same time.

## 6. THE THIRD REGISTER, AND IT IS THE CLEANEST IDEA IN THIS VERSION

Confirmed 11.8.2026 by Marko when the laptop screen came back as real marathon footage rather than as a
drawing, which was the right answer and is now a law.

**The film has two visual registers and they divide along the line the film is actually about.**

    PHOTOGRAPHIC     what is true. Manan, his room, his desk, his hands, and the
                     real footage of the real runner on his laptop screen. Evidence.

    DRAWN            what is thought. The 1923 room, the little runner on the desk,
                     Coach Brain, the lever, the curve. Explanation, model, theory.

**So the film separates evidence from explanation without ever saying so**, and a viewer absorbs the
distinction in the first twenty seconds without being taught it. That is worth more to a science
competition than any amount of production polish, because it is the scientific method drawn as a visual
rule.

**And it pays off exactly where the story needs it.** When the drawn runner leaves the book and comes
out onto the real desk in scene 3, a thing from the world of explanation walks into the world of
evidence, which is precisely what the crack is. When Manan's real hand takes hold of the drawn lever in
scene 5, a thing from the world of evidence reaches into the world of explanation. The two crossings are
the two turns of the film, and both of them are only legible because the registers were kept apart from
the first frame.

**Practical rule.** If it is a fact, photograph it. If it is a model of a fact, draw it. Nothing sits in
both worlds except at the two crossings, and those are earned.

## 7. THE POP UP BOOK, WHICH IS WHAT THE DRAWN WORLD ACTUALLY IS

Discovered in the frame rather than designed, 11.8.2026. `V5_2_2` was asked for as a small drawn room
standing on the page like a model, and what came back is unmistakably **a pop up book**: the drawn room
folds up out of the gutter as a standing panel with the floor of the room lying flat on the page in
front of it, exactly as a paper engineer would build it.

**Keep it, because it explains the whole film in an object a child already understands.** A pop up book
is a flat thing that becomes a place when you open it, which is precisely what a scientific model is and
precisely what this film is doing. Nobody has to be told. Everybody has held one.

**And it solves problems we had not solved.**

    the register       a pop up is paper, so it can never be mistaken for the real
                       world. Evidence and explanation stay separate by construction.
    the scale          it is small because pop ups are small. No argument needed.
    the shadow         a standing paper panel casts an obvious shadow onto the page,
                       which is the join between the two worlds, free.
    the crossing       a runner leaving a pop up and walking onto the desk is a
                       far stronger image than a drawing leaving a drawing.
    the animation      the animator is drawing paper mechanics, folds and hinges
                       and tabs, which is cheaper and more charming than free
                       floating illustration.

**So the drawn world of version five is a pop up book, and every drawn element obeys paper.** Coach
Brain steps off a folded page. The lever stands in a slot cut into the page. The runner runs down the
flat part of the spread and onto the wood. What was a visual style is now a physical law with rules the
audience can feel.
