**SCENE 5, THE CRACK. 0:49 to 0:58. Nine seconds, three frames.**

Opened 11.8.2026. **DECIDED THE SAME DAY: OPTION TWO. The contradiction arrives as the runner.** Marko
chose it off a storyboard sheet, filed at `assets/V4/attempts/S5_board_option_two_chosen_full.jpg`.

**The three frames, taken straight off that board.**

    5A   The runner seen through the window from inside the room, small and far
         away, sprinting. The room is dim, the window is bright.
    5B   The runner large and close, filling the frame, mid stride, head back,
         accelerating. The turn.
    5C   The man at the window with his back to us, watching him, a single sheet
         of paper hanging forgotten from one hand.

**And the runner is OUR runner, not a man of 1923.** He is the marathon runner from `RUNNER.jpg`, vest,
shorts, modern shoes, the number **27** on his back, the same man who collapses in scene 1 and is
released in scene 8. A runner from the present appearing outside a window in 1923 is not a mistake, it is
**the crack itself**: the scientist is looking at something that cannot be there. It also plants the
return of the runner before scene 8 needs him, which is `storytelling.md`'s rule about planting before it
is needed.

**Which is why the scene is not about a man being wrong.** He is looking at the impossible, calmly and
carefully, the way a good scientist does. Nobody is foolish. The theory simply does not reach far enough,
and the audience feels the gap instead of being told about it.

**5C is the handoff to scene 6.** The paper hanging from his hand is the theory becoming dead weight, and
the next scene leaves the room and climbs the body into the head.

---

## THE PROBLEM WITH THIS SCENE, WHICH WAS DIAGNOSED BEFORE IT WAS WRITTEN

`elements.md` calls this **the thinnest scene in the film** and it is right. As currently conceived it is
a man looking at a thing he cannot explain, which is pure **wind**: idea, question, structure, and
nothing underneath it. No weight, no feeling. A scene made of one element is admired and not felt.

It also carries the heaviest job in the film's argument. This is where Hill stops being enough. It has to
be done **without blame**, because `storytelling.md` forbids a culprit: he is not wrong, he is
incomplete, and the audience must feel the incompleteness rather than be told it. A film that scores a
point off a dead scientist loses the jury and deserves to.

---

## OPTION ONE. THE CONTRADICTION ARRIVES ON PAPER

He has his curve. A second sheet does not agree with it. The crack is inside his own evidence and the
scene never leaves the room.

**For it.** Cheap, safe, no new geometry, no new character, and it keeps the laboratory sealed, which
makes the escape in scene 6 stronger. It is also honest to how science actually breaks: on paper,
quietly, before anybody says anything.

**Against it.** It is still pure wind. Two sheets of paper and a face. It adds no weight and it risks
being the least interesting nine seconds in the film, sitting immediately before the two best scenes.

---

## OPTION TWO. THE CONTRADICTION ARRIVES AS THE RUNNER

The drawn runner from scene 1 comes back, through the window or across the page, still accelerating after
the oxygen is gone. The man cannot explain him.

**For it.** It puts a **body** back into a room made of paper, which is exactly the missing element:
earth, and a little fire. It makes the crack a person rather than a discrepancy, which is what the film
is about, since the whole thesis is that the thing science was missing is a human being. And it plants
the return of the runner before scene 8 needs it.

**Against it.** It is a bigger idea and a bigger spend, and it risks making the scientist look foolish,
which is forbidden. He must be watching something genuinely impossible, not failing to notice something
obvious.

## WHAT IS NOT IN DOUBT

The room is `V4_4A`'s room and it is not rebuilt. Whatever is chosen, the scene is derived from the
locked scene 4 frames in the editor, and the wall can be redressed with new work without disturbing
anything else.

The scene ends unresolved. The film admits that the answer does not exist yet, and that discomfort is
deliberate and is what makes scene 6 possible.
