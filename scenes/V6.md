## THE BOARDING METHOD FOR THIS PASS, AND WHY IT IS DIFFERENT

**One sheet per scene, six panels, generated on Nano Banana Pro rather than a fast sketch model.** Eight
renders, about three minutes each. The whole film visible in under half an hour instead of over days.

**And the finding that made it possible is one we already paid for. Sheets beget sheets.** A multi panel
reference imposes its grid on the output, which was a problem when single frames were wanted and is
exactly the behaviour required here. Feed a six panel board, get a six panel board.

**Two references per scene, no more.**

    R1   a CAST STRIP, built in the container, the characters and objects
         of that scene cropped side by side onto one image. Holds the
         likenesses. Files in assets/V6/cast/
    R2   an approved six panel storyboard sheet. Holds the grid, the
         pencil hand and the lettering.

**Why a cast strip rather than several sheets.** The tool takes a small number of references and a full
model sheet spends its attention on views we do not need. A strip carries only the one view of each
character the scene actually uses, so nothing is wasted and continuity holds across all eight sheets
because every sheet is built from the same crops.
