# THE NEW DIALOGUE, AFTER THE HOUSE IS WIRED

Baba, 3.9.2026. For approval. Nothing filed into the drama yet.

**Why it changes.** The corridor now shows six rooms, each with a probe and a
fibre running to one place. Coach Brain's lines were written before that existed,
so he explains things the audience has already been shown, and one of the five
things he claims to read is not a physiological signal at all.

---

## THE ONE WORD THAT WAS WRONG

Current line:

> Pulse, breath, temperature, water, distance. I read all of it, the whole time.

**Distance is not a signal the body sends.** It is something a runner knows, not
something a brain measures. Every other item on that list is a real afferent.
Replace it with the one an audience already half believes in and that Noakes
disproved most directly:

> Pulse, breath, temperature, water, **sugar**. I read all of it, the whole time.

## THE BOARDS, REWRITTEN

    MANAN
      Nineteen twenty three. Hill puts runners on a treadmill and measures
      everything they do. His answer is simple. Fatigue is in the muscle.

    MANAN
      You run. The muscle uses up its oxygen. The fuel is spent. And when the
      tank reads empty, the body stops.

    COACH BRAIN
      Pulse, breath, temperature, water, sugar. I read all of it, the whole
      time. That is what every one of those cables you walked past is for.

    COACH BRAIN
      And I ask one question with it. Can we carry on safely?

    COACH BRAIN
      When the answer starts to look like no, I slow you down. Not because
      anything has run out. Because of where it is heading.

    MANAN
      Heading.

    COACH BRAIN
      I do not wait for the tank to empty. I watch how fast it is emptying, and
      I stop you while there is still enough left to get you home. So no.
      Fatigue is in the brain, not in your legs. Up here. And it decides long
      before anything is actually in danger.

    COACH BRAIN
      When I stop you, the fuel is still there.

    MANAN
      It was. I saw it. The sugar cellar was full. The fat store was barely
      touched. The fibres were not torn.

    COACH BRAIN
      No. They never are.

    MANAN
      So the wall...

    COACH BRAIN
      Is a number I chose. Carefully. To keep you alive.

    MANAN
      Then it can be a different number.

## WHAT CHANGED, AND WHY EACH ONE

**Sugar replaces distance.** As above. The only outright factual correction.

**"That is what every one of those cables you walked past is for."** One line
that ties the control room to the corridor. Without it the wiring is decoration;
with it, the walk was evidence.

**"I watch how fast it is emptying."** This is the actual mechanism and it was
missing. The central governor is not a low fuel warning, it is anticipatory: it
reads the RATE and stops you early enough that you survive the finish. Say that
and Coach Brain stops sounding like a fuse and starts sounding like a pilot.

**Manan gets to answer from what he saw.** *The sugar cellar was full. The fat
store was barely touched. The fibres were not torn.* Three rooms, named back.
The boy now proves the point himself instead of being told it, which is the
film's own law, and it pays off six frames of walking.

**"No. They never are."** Coach Brain admits it flatly and without triumph. He
is not a villain and this is the moment it has to be obvious.

## THE CROATIAN

    COACH BRAIN
      Puls, dah, temperatura, voda, šećer. Čitam sve to, cijelo vrijeme. Za to
      služi svaki onaj kabel pored kojeg si prošao.

    COACH BRAIN
      Ne čekam da spremnik bude prazan. Gledam koliko se brzo prazni, i
      zaustavim te dok još ima dovoljno da te vrati kući.

    MANAN
      Bilo ga je. Vidio sam. Podrum sa šećerom je bio pun. Zaliha masti jedva
      dirnuta. Vlakna nisu bila potrgana.

    COACH BRAIN
      Ne. Nikad nisu.
