The prizes

There is one winner. No second and no third place, and no cash for runners up. The package is worth four hundred thousand dollars and it is split three ways.

The student$250,000 post secondary scholarship
The teacher$50,000 prize for the teacher who inspired the entry
The school$100,000 Breakthrough Science Lab

Beyond the single winner there is recognition rather than money. Up to thirty videos reach the popular vote, made of the top two from each of seven geographic regions plus the highest scoring entries from the evaluation panel. Those are posted across the Breakthrough channels for sixteen days and the most liked is named Popular Vote Top Scorer.

The rules that can disqualify us

  • Two minutes maximum. No more than 2:00 on the YouTube timer. Ours runs to exactly 2:00, so the export has to be checked frame by frame, not approximately.
  • One entry per person, under one identity. Multiple entries can be disqualified.
  • Age. At least 13 by 11 May 2026 and not yet 19 by 1 October 2026.
  • English only.
  • Individual entry. The entrant is the student. Crew and mentors are permitted, the entry belongs to Manan.
  • Submitted as a YouTube link inside the application form, not as a file.
  • Peer review is mandatory for scholarship eligibility. Five entries scored by 30 September.
  • The entry becomes the property of the sponsor on submission and is not returned.

How it is judged

Four criteria, weighted equally in effect. Engagement, how compelling the explanation is. Illumination, how clearly the idea is explained. Creativity, originality of the presentation. Difficulty, the complexity of the idea chosen.

What this means for our schedule

Venkatesh being free from 18 August rather than 15 August costs us three days and we have twenty eight days of post production after that. It is not a problem, and it is better than a rushed shoot.

The revised shape. Documents to both departments on Monday 10 August. Animation begins immediately, since it does not wait on the shoot. Camera on Tuesday 18 August, one day, second day held in reserve. Composite and edit through to the end of August. Sound, music and grade in the first week of September. Lock by 10 September, which leaves five clear days of margin before the deadline.

Two things that must not slip. The upload has to happen days early, not on the night, because the entry is a YouTube link and processing plus a form failure on the last evening is how films miss deadlines. And the peer review of five other entries has to be done before 30 September or the scholarship eligibility is lost no matter how good the film is.
